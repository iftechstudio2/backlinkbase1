Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 20, 2026

1. About this policy

This Privacy Policy explains how BacklinkBase handles information in connection with https://backlinkbase.org/. It is intended to provide clear information for visitors and website submitters.

2. Information you provide

If you submit a website, information may include the URL, selected category, title and description. A submission may also result in website metadata being processed, such as a page title, description, logo/favicon or Open Graph information.

3. Technical information

Technical information may be processed for security, abuse prevention, rate limiting and operation of the service. This can include browser/client identifiers, hashed technical identifiers, request information and timestamps.

4. Local storage

BacklinkBase may use browser local storage for functions such as remembering a browser-generated client identifier, preventing repeated votes from the same browser, and retaining local directory data for site functionality.

5. Third-party services

Automatic website details may be requested from a third-party metadata service. Third-party services operate under their own policies and BacklinkBase does not control their independent processing.

6. Advertising

BacklinkBase may use advertising services, including Google AdSense, if advertising is enabled. This policy does not state that advertising is currently active unless advertising code is actually deployed. If Google advertising is used, Google and its partners may use cookies or similar technologies subject to applicable settings, policies and consent requirements.

7. Public directory information

Information submitted for a website listing, including URL, title, description, category and related public metadata, may be displayed publicly after review. Do not submit personal or confidential information that you do not want published.

8. Use of information

Information may be used to operate the directory, process submissions, display listings, moderate comments, prevent spam and abuse, maintain security, troubleshoot problems, improve the service and comply with applicable obligations.

9. Third-party websites

Directory listings link to websites operated by third parties. Their privacy practices and data handling are outside BacklinkBase's control.

10. Data retention and security

Information is retained as reasonably necessary for listings, moderation, security, dispute handling and legal obligations. Reasonable technical and organizational measures are used, but no internet service can guarantee absolute security.

11. Privacy rights

Depending on where you live and which laws apply, you may have rights concerning access, correction, deletion, restriction, objection, portability or consent withdrawal. Requests should be made through the contact@backlinkbase.org.

12. Changes

This policy may be updated when the website, service providers, advertising configuration or applicable requirements change.
