Frequently Asked Questions

Everything you need to know about getting your website listed on BacklinkBase.

Is submitting my website completely free?
Yes. Standard inclusion in the BacklinkBase directory is 100% free with no mandatory reciprocal links or hidden costs.
How long does the editorial review take?
Our moderation team reviews pending submissions within 24 to 48 hours to ensure content quality and verify safety compliance.
What types of websites are accepted?
We curate functional web apps, SaaS platforms, developer tools, blogs, educational portals, and genuine digital services. Submissions with spam, malware, or broken domains will be rejected.
How does a directory listing help my SEO?
A verified listing provides search engine crawlers with an authoritative reference pointing directly to your canonical domain, aiding search indexation and brand trust.
Can I update my listing information after approval?
Yes. If your branding or URL changes, contact our team via the Contact page with your domain details to update the record.
