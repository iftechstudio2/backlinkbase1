Submission Guidelines

BacklinkBase is a hand-curated directory dedicated to showcasing high-quality web tools, software products, SaaS, and digital resources. Please review our submission criteria to ensure your listing is approved quickly.

1. Quality Standards & Eligibility

To maintain directory standards and genuine SEO discovery value, submitted websites must satisfy the following baseline requirements:

  • Active & Functional: The website must be live, responsive, accessible via HTTPS (valid SSL), and functional. Under-construction pages or parked domains are rejected.
  • Substantial Value: The site must offer genuine utility, products, services, tooling, educational content, or digital solutions for web users.
  • Original Content: We do not accept automated scrape aggregators, AI-spun thin affiliate doorway pages, or copycat websites.
  • Professional Presentation: Clean UI design, readable typography, functional navigation, and transparent contact or about information.

2. What We Strictly Reject

Submissions containing or promoting any of the following are permanently banned from our index:

  • Malware, spyware, phishing, exploits, cracked software, or unauthorized pirated downloads.
  • Deceptive financial schemes, unregulated crypto scams, or high-risk gambling platforms.
  • Adult, sexually explicit, or violent/hateful material.
  • Doorway pages created solely to funnel search traffic into low-grade affiliate redirects.
  • Duplicate submissions of the same root domain or multiple subdomains pointing to identical content.

3. Submission Data Best Practices

When filling out the Submit Form, follow these editorial guidelines:

  • Website URL: Provide your canonical root domain or main landing page URL (e.g., https://yourdomain.com).
  • Title: Use your official brand or tool name (e.g., “Stripe”, not “Best Payment Gateway Online Free Cheap Stripe”). Avoid excessive ALL CAPS and keyword stuffing.
  • Description: Write a concise, factual summary (1 to 3 sentences) explaining what your tool or company does and who it helps. Avoid exaggerated buzzwords.
  • Category Selection: Choose the single most accurate category from our 70+ topic options. If your product spans multiple areas, pick its primary purpose.

4. Review Process & Timeline

Every submission is queued for manual inspection by our moderation team. Standard review takes approximately 24 to 48 hours. If your website meets all criteria, it is marked as approved and immediately appears in the directory and category listings. If rejected, no low-quality backlink is created.

5. Updating an Existing Listing

If your product has rebranded, changed domains, updated descriptions, or if you need to request a category re-assignment, please email our editorial desk at contact@backlinkbase.org with your live listing domain and desired changes.
